Steam Deck audio: the Vangogh SOF firmware (lib/firmware/amd/sof*) and the
acp5x / sof-nau8821-max UCM profiles that route the Deck's speaker DSP
chain - without them the speakers are quiet and tinny. Mirrored from the
gaming-distro packaging of Valve's SteamOS files; resync from
https://github.com/batocera-linux/batocera.linux/tree/master/package/batocera/audio/steamdeck-dsp
